The sign-off service of SPEC Section 8.3 synthesises the submitted RTL with the GPDK045 library and simulates the netlists with SDF back-annotation against its own testbench, then publishes a scorecard on the branch `check-results`. Two submissions were made. `check/400` (commit 60ed005) carried the 400 MHz design while `rtl_800/` was still empty, and `check/800` (commit b6917e9) carried both final designs, which are unchanged since.

At the time this report was built, the branch `check-results` did not exist on the remote (`git fetch origin check-results` returned "couldn't find remote ref"), so no GPDK045 scorecard was available to compare with. The service is described as answering within 5 to 20 minutes while it is online, and the absence of any result branch suggests it was offline during the session. The comparison therefore cannot be made here. The open-source results above are complete and stand on their own.

Some differences should be expected once a scorecard is available. GPDK045 sign-off runs at its own corner, typically nominal, while this flow uses the Nangate45 slow corner at 0.95 V and 125 °C, where gates are roughly two to three times slower. So the GPDK045 slack of both designs should be much larger, and the 800 MHz design, sized for the slow corner, should close with a wide margin. GPDK045 also has different flip-flops and a different synthesis tool, so area and power will not match the Nangate figures cell for cell. The functional results should agree, since both implementations are fully synchronous and every output except `EN_readMem` and `memVal_data` comes from a register.
